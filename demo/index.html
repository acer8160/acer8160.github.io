What you are looking at

A practice take recorded inside Your Metronome with the front camera. The visual beat, the tempo and the click all end up inside the exported file, which is saved straight to Photos. Nothing here was edited afterwards.

This is not a screen recording. The app knows every click time to the millisecond, so the overlay is rebuilt as Core Animation layers and composited with AVFoundation — frame-accurate in both portrait and landscape, all on device, with no upload and no account.
